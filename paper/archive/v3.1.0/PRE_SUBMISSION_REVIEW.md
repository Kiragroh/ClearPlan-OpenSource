# Pre-submission review

Review date: July 30, 2026

The manuscript and release candidate were reviewed independently from five
perspectives: medical physics and clinical workflow, software architecture and
reproducibility, radiotherapy safety and regulation, scientific novelty and
literature positioning, and journal-facing editorial compliance. A separate
adversarial pass then challenged the principal claims and release evidence.

## Resolved review bundles

1. **Immutable software evidence.** Version metadata, release notes, deterministic
   packaging, SHA-256 output, and a clean-clone verification procedure are part
   of the v3.1.0 release workflow.
2. **Traceability.** The manuscript's numerical statements are linked to
   checked-in scenarios, builders, tests, and machine-readable evidence in
   `TRACEABILITY_MATRIX.md`.
3. **Claim boundaries.** Results are restricted to software verification and a
   deterministic synthetic demonstration. No clinical sensitivity,
   specificity, workflow, safety, or outcome claim is made.
4. **TPS transfer boundary.** The Eclipse/ESAPI host, patient-free simulator,
   and illustrative RayStation producer are distinguished explicitly. The
   RayStation example is not presented as a released UI integration or a
   clinically commissioned adapter.
5. **DVH and PQM semantics.** Dose-volume notation, interpolation, near-maximum
   dose terminology, units, thresholds, and status precedence are defined and
   exercised at boundary conditions.
6. **Positioning.** The introduction and comparison table distinguish ClearPlan
   from prior single-TPS chart-check implementations, knowledge-based DVH
   comparison services, and hybrid automated/manual review platforms.
7. **Commissioning and governance.** Public regression fixtures are separated
   from site-owned clinical acceptance tests, mappings, thresholds, and release
   approval in `docs/local-commissioning-scaffold.md`.
8. **Submission compliance.** The author order, affiliations, correspondence
   details, CRediT statement, ethics, funding, competing-interest,
   acknowledgements, generative-AI disclosure, figures, tables, abstract, and
   keywords are represented in the editable manuscript.
