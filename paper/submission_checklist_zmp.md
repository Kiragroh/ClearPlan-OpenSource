# ZMP author-review and submission checklist

Target: *Zeitschrift für Medizinische Physik*. Requested article type: Technical
Note. Language: American English. This checklist does not assert submission.

## Prepared study content

- [x] Supplied five-author order, two postal affiliations, corresponding contact,
  ORCID, funding/interest statements, CRediT roles, and AI-use disclosure.
- [x] Exclusively synthetic study; native interoperability work kept separate.
- [x] Two figures, two editable tables, 21 references, and a synthetic active-plan
  report supplement with an offline HTML companion.
- [x] PAM weighting and target choice, dual-layer geometry, CI/reciprocal, GI/HI,
  and the estimated—not measured—rate trace described with their limits.
- [x] Versioned local configuration and separately confirmed ARIA document write
  distinguished from read-only TPS access.
- [x] Fresh software evidence: 385 Core, 20 synthetic DICOM, 20 illustrative
  RayStation, and nine stock-parser tests passed; one private-source stock group
  explicitly skipped. No clinical-performance inference.
- [x] Named software revision and exact SHA-256 of Supplement S1 in manuscript.

## Required author/editorial actions before journal submission

- [ ] Every coauthor reviews the complete manuscript, verifies their CRediT roles,
  and approves the exact portal-generated submission PDF.
- [ ] Confirm the current portal article category and author-guide requirements,
  including length, abstract, artwork, disclosure, and supplementary-file limits.
  The current online guide could not be retrieved during this revision; no
  numerical journal limit is asserted here.
- [ ] Confirm originality, absence of simultaneous submission, and any applicable
  institutional permission before sending the cover letter.
- [ ] Obtain local clinical commissioning independently of this software study.

Public release publication/readback, artifact hashes, visual review, and
manuscript-audit outcomes are recorded separately. This checklist is not their
substitute and does not claim that an editor has accepted the paper.
